HR Employee Attrition Analysis

End-to-end attrition prediction on 1,470 IBM HR records — class imbalance handled with SMOTE, three models benchmarked (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost), and the final XGBoost model tuned via GridSearchCV for recall over accuracy, since missing a departing employee costs far more than a false alarm. SHAP explains every prediction. Overtime employees turned out 3x more likely to leave. Results feed a 3-page Power BI dashboard scoring all 1,470 employees and flagging 435 as high-risk.

SuperStore Sales Analysis

Exploratory analysis of 51,290 global retail orders (2011–2014) across 7 markets. Revenue grew 90% over the period, but 22% of orders carry discounts above 20% — directly causing losses of up to $99/order. EMEA is the weakest market at 5.4% margin against an 11.6% company average, while Home Office is the fastest-growing segment at +119.5%. Findings translated into ranked business recommendations, not just charts.
